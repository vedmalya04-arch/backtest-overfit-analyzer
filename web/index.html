Extreme Value Theory (EVT) Hurdle Rate

$$E\Big[\max_{n=1..N} \{SR_n\}\Big] \approx \sqrt{V[SR]} \cdot \left[ (1 - \gamma)\,\Phi^{-1}\left(1 - \frac{1}{N}\right) + \gamma\,\Phi^{-1}\left(1 - \frac{1}{N e}\right) \right]$$
$$\text{Asymptotic Expansion: } E[\max SR] \approx \sqrt{V[SR]} \cdot \left[ \sqrt{2\ln N} + \frac{\gamma}{\sqrt{2\ln N}} \right]$$

Quantifies the expected maximum Sharpe ratio obtained by pure selection bias across N backtested trials. Even when true strategy alpha is zero, testing multiple parameter combinations guarantees lucky false discoveries that expand with $\sqrt{2\ln N}$.

$N$: Total Trials Tested
$V[SR]$: Variance Across Trials
$\gamma$: Euler-Mascheroni Constant (0.5772...)
$\Phi^{-1}$: Inverse Standard Normal CDF
SHARPE ESTIMATION VARIANCE Statistics.cpp

Bailey & López de Prado Variance Formula

$$V[SR] = \frac{1 - \gamma_3\cdot SR^* + \left(\frac{\gamma_4 - 1}{4}\right)\cdot(SR^*)^2}{T - 1}$$

Standard Sharpe estimation assumes independent normal returns. This formula computes the exact asymptotic variance of the Sharpe ratio after penalizing for sample length (T), negative skewness ($\gamma_3$), and fat-tailed excess kurtosis ($\gamma_4$).

$T$: Track Record Length (Days)
$SR^*$: Candidate Reported Sharpe
$\gamma_3$: Return Sample Skewness
$\gamma_4$: Sample Kurtosis (Normal = 3.0)
